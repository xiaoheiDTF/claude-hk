# 003-4-issue-claim

Use this skill when you need to claim an issue before working on it.

Rules:
1. Claim the issue early to avoid duplicate work.
2. Record the claim in the issue thread and follow the repo's label conventions.
3. Keep the claim status visible until the work is finished or released to another agent.
